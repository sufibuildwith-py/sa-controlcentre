# Payroll policy and immutability

SA Command stores money only as signed integer minor units. ₹3,000 is persisted and transported as `300000`; floating point is never used for salary arithmetic.

The default demo strategy is `PER_WORKING_DAY` with 26 configured working days. An `ABSENT` record contributes one unpaid day and `HALF_DAY` contributes 0.5; other attendance states contribute zero. The deduction is `base salary × unpaid days ÷ working days`, rounded once to the nearest minor unit with `HALF_UP`. The alternative `NONE` policy makes no attendance deduction. These are demonstration defaults, not a claim about SA Production's final HR policy; both are isolated behind `PayrollCalculationPolicy` and configured in `application.yml` or environment variables.

Adjustments require a reason. BONUS, OVERTIME, DEDUCTION and ADVANCE accept positive magnitudes. CORRECTION and OTHER may be positive or negative. An advance is money already received by the employee, so it reduces the current amount owed. Net payable is the snapshotted base plus bonus, overtime and positive corrections, less attendance deductions, advances, deductions and negative corrections.

Every transfer of money is an immutable `payroll_payments` ledger entry with its amount, effective date/time, method, optional reference and note, recorder, and creation timestamp. `totalPaid` is the sum of these entries; `remaining` is `max(netPayable - totalPaid, 0)`. A payroll item is UNPAID, PARTIALLY_PAID or PAID from those values. Overpayment is rejected and payment deletion is not exposed. V1.1 does not provide payment reversal; a future correction must use an explicit auditable reversal entry rather than deletion.

The state machine is `DRAFT → CALCULATED → APPROVED → PAID → LOCKED`. Recalculation and adjustment are permitted only while CALCULATED. Payments are recorded while APPROVED, and the period becomes PAID only when every employee has zero remaining balance. The fully-paid transition emits one salary notification per employee; partial payments do not emit duplicates. Only PAID can be locked. LOCKED is terminal: later employee salary or attendance changes cannot modify the salary snapshot, adjustments, or payment history.
